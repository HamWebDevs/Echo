# Install and play the Android preview

Developer: **HamWebDev**. Working title: **Project ECHO**.

1. Download `project-echo-preview.apk` to an Android 9 or newer phone.
2. Open the downloaded APK and choose Install. If Android asks, allow that browser or file manager to install this downloaded app; you can switch that permission off afterward.
3. Open Project ECHO and choose **Play Endless**. The short tutorial explains the controls.
4. Drag in the lower control pad to steer. Collect gold sparks before the charge expires. Release your thumb to keep your current direction.
5. After eight seconds, an echo starts replaying your earlier route. Avoid it and the arena boundary. Every additional echo raises your spark multiplier.
6. After a run, use the replay to see what happened, retry immediately, or share your score card.

Try **The Daily Signal** for a repeatable daily spark sequence and **Collection** for looks unlocked with lifetime sparks. Both modes work offline. Progress is stored on the device; uninstalling removes it. An interrupted unfinished run is not recovered.

This preview uses a development signing key. Purchases are unavailable until the owner's Google Play and RevenueCat configuration is connected. The supplied unsigned AAB is for release preparation, not direct installation. The app has not been published to Google Play.

For useful feedback, record your phone model and Android version, whether the controls made sense without coaching, what ended your first run, and whether you chose to retry. Physical-device performance and independent player testing remain necessary before launch.
