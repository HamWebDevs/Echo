The game and your device

Project ECHO's core gameplay works offline. Scores, earned cosmetics, tutorial progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. No player account is required. There is no chat, public leaderboard or advertising in this version. The app does not request access to contacts, microphone, camera or precise device location.

Local progress is not synchronized between devices. Removing the app removes its local progress. Purchased content can be restored through the same Google Play account.

Optional usage analytics

Analytics is off by default. If you enable Usage analytics in Settings in a configured release, the game sends usage events to Google Firebase Analytics: tutorial progress, run starts and endings, scores, sparks, duration, death causes, retries and sharing actions. The game does not send your movement history through this analytics integration.

Firebase may process standard app-instance identifiers, app/device characteristics and general regional information as described in Firebase's privacy documentation. Analytics helps HamWebDev find problems and understand which parts of the game are useful. Turning the setting off stops future game usage events through this integration. Platform diagnostics controlled by your Android settings are separate.

Optional purchases

Google Play handles payment details. RevenueCat validates purchases and manages anonymous customer identifiers and purchase entitlements so the permanent Supporter Pack can be restored. HamWebDev does not receive your payment card details through the app. Purchase services require an internet connection and may process data under their own terms. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Sharing

Sharing is initiated by you. The app creates a score-card image and passes it to the destination you choose in the Android share sheet. It does not upload your score card automatically.

Retention, choices and requests

Local records remain until you remove the app or its app data. Service providers retain purchase and analytics data according to the configured service policies and applicable obligations. HamWebDev must document its final analytics retention setting before this policy is published.

You can disable optional analytics in Settings. To make a privacy request or ask about service-held information, contact HamWebDev using the support email above. Avoid including payment credentials or unnecessary personal information.

Audience and updates

The game is intended for older teens and adults and is not directed at young children. HamWebDev may update this policy when features or services change. A current version will be available at the published policy URL and through the store listing.
